# Homepage (and site-wide world)

Scope: homepage first, then every page (Buy, Sell, Neighborhoods, city pages, Blog, post, About, Contact). Mode: Persuade.
Audience and action: first-time buyers and relocators (sellers second) deciding whether to trust Muzamil; action is text, call, or book a 30-minute call.
Proof on hand: real MLS stats for four cities, four real reviews, real photos. No invented claims.
Constraints: confirmed nav (Buy, Sell, Neighborhoods, Blog, About, Contact); blog grouping C; light theme; real photos only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The East Bay read like a park trail map: the climb from flats to hills is the price ladder, and each city is a marked trailhead with its numbers. Refuses the stock agent site (headshot hero, search bar, carousel).
OWN-WORLD: Pale map-paper ground with faint sage contour linework, deep green-black ink, one trail-marker orange, a distinct trail color per city reused on its page, playbook, and posts. Huge numerals over small labels in one face; legend boxes as cards; thin trail lines as dividers.
STORY: The visitor sees the four cities and what winning there takes, believes Muzamil reads both the house and the market, and texts or books.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, name, one-line promise, Text me (primary) and Book a call. Right, his real photo. Behind both, faint contours; across the bottom, four trailhead markers with median and percent over asking.
FORM: Trail Map, assigned candidate 6 of 7, seed key 2fe1cc1d. Raises: hierarchy by size alone (from type specimen); same four numbers in the same order for every city (from instrument panel).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
